Local face models used by GYM OS

Human 3.3.6: Vladimir Mandic, MIT (LICENSE-HUMAN.txt).
Model weights are converted/quantized by Human; original provenance remains
in each model JSON. Models are not retrained by GYM OS.

blazeface: Google MediaPipe BlazeFace, Apache 2.0.
https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe
faceres: Andrey Savchenko / HSE_FaceRec_tf, Apache 2.0.
https://github.com/av-savchenko/HSE_FaceRec_tf
liveness: leokwu/livenessnet, Apache 2.0.
https://github.com/leokwu/livenessnet

See the accompanying upstream license files. Human model provenance:
https://github.com/vladmandic/human/wiki/Models

The optional Kaggle antispoof weights are not distributed or enabled.
Camera identification is optional and needs explicit member consent.
A liveness model is a heuristic, not proof of identity or a certified
anti-spoofing system. Accuracy and thresholds must be checked on the
customer's camera and lighting before camera attendance is relied upon.
Use a card instead when matching is unclear. No camera video is saved.
